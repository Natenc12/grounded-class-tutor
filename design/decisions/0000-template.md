- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN | accepted — amends NNNN

> Once a decision is `accepted`, its wording can still be copy-edited in place, but the
> **decision itself changes only via a new ADR** that names the one it revises. Two shapes:
> wholesale replacement (`superseded by NNNN`) and partial (`amends NNNN` — say which claim
> changes and what stands; see `0024-retriever-score-clamp-amends-0017.md`). The revised ADR's
> own `Status:` line then gets the matching back-pointer (`<claim> amended YYYY-MM-DD by
> ADR NNNN`) — that edit is the sanctioned copy-edit — so a reader never has to read git
> history to find out what a decision currently is.
